Check a spot in Street View before you go. Set your route before you drive: using a hand-held phone at the wheel is illegal, and so is using one while you supervise a learner.

Mock tests are practice routes made up from the spots here, not real test routes. Driving times are estimates. Distances in the spot list are in a straight line, and spots are picked from OpenStreetMap data, so treat them as suggestions.
